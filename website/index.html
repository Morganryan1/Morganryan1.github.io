About Me

I’m Morgan M. Ryan, MA — a GIS Analyst and Researcher with a passion for geospatial data, automation, and applied geography. My work integrates Python scripting, web cartography, and GIS system design to streamline data-driven decision-making and spatial analysis.

I currently serve as a Senior GIS Analyst at Philadelphia Gas Works, where I lead automation and data integrity initiatives across the Esri Enterprise ecosystem. Previously, I’ve worked as a Solutions Engineer at Geographic Consulting Group International, supporting Esri Utility Network adoption for U.S. military installations, and as a Senior IT Applications Developer within Nebraska’s Office of the Chief Information Officer, where I built automation tools for statewide GIS operations.

My professional experience spans GIS automation, data modeling, and web application development, with an emphasis on enhancing accessibility and efficiency in enterprise environments. I’ve developed and maintained GNSS field programs, implemented automated quality checks, and built dashboards that connect data insights to decision-makers.

Before entering the professional sector, I earned my Master of Arts in Geography from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, where my thesis, “Reexamining the Desert: A Study of Place-Based Food Insecurity,” expanded USDA’s food desert methodology using mixed-method and place-based analysis. During my graduate studies, I also served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant, teaching GIS and web mapping using ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, and JavaScript libraries such as Leaflet and D3.js.

My academic and professional journey reflects a deep curiosity for how geography, data, and human behavior intersect. In the future, I plan to pursue a PhD in Geography to further explore the relationships between place, access, and spatial inequity through computational and web-based geographies.

Outside of work, I enjoy community engagement, mentoring emerging GIS professionals, and exploring new methods of spatial storytelling through design and technology.
