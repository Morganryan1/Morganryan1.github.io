About Me

I’m Morgan M. Ryan, MA — a GIS Analyst, researcher, and web cartographer with a passion for spatial analysis, data automation, and applied geography. My work bridges the technical and conceptual sides of GIS, emphasizing scalable solutions, accessible data, and meaningful map design.

I currently serve as a Senior GIS Analyst at Philadelphia Gas Works, where I develop and maintain enterprise GIS systems using Python, JavaScript, and ArcGIS Enterprise. My work focuses on automation, field data integration, and creating analytical tools that support operational efficiency and decision-making.

Before joining PGW, I held roles as a Solutions Engineer with the Geographic Consulting Group International and a Senior IT Applications Developer at the Nebraska Office of the CIO. Across these positions, I’ve implemented Utility Network models for U.S. military installations, built geospatial automation frameworks for state agencies, and led enterprise data modernization initiatives.

My academic foundation includes a Master of Arts in Geography from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, where my thesis “Reexamining the Desert: A Study of Place-Based Food Insecurity” developed new methodologies for identifying food deserts through spatial and qualitative analysis. My research experience spans food geography, health accessibility, and human-environment interaction.

While I’m focused on advancing my technical and applied experience in geospatial systems, I plan to eventually pursue a Ph.D. in Geography to deepen my research in place-based modeling, social geography, and spatial data science.

Outside of professional work, I remain active in the academic and community geography space—having served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant, Geography Representative to the Graduate Student Association, and Fit4Lincoln educator. I continue to enjoy connecting academic research with real-world applications in environmental and social contexts.
