Automated PNG-to-Polygon Conversion

ArcGIS ModelBuilder · Python · Spatial Automation

Developed during my internship with the Nebraska Department of Transportation (ROW Division), this ArcGIS ModelBuilder workflow converts scanned right-of-way project images into clean, usable GIS data. The model automates reclassification, polygon extraction, and topological cleanup — turning hours of manual digitizing into a consistent, repeatable process.
