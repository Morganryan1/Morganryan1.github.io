Current Role: Senior GIS Analyst – Philadelphia Gas Works

At Philadelphia Gas Works (PGW), I lead geospatial data initiatives that support critical infrastructure management, field operations, and regulatory compliance across the city’s natural gas network. My work focuses on building automated, data-driven solutions that enhance accuracy, speed, and collaboration within PGW’s enterprise GIS ecosystem. Because most of my work includes PII and secured data, I can not provide live applications, however, I can show images of what some of my work looks like.

A major focus of my role is automating data collection and cleaning processes using Python, JavaScript, and ArcGIS Enterprise tools. These automations reduce manual data entry, detect and correct geometry or schema inconsistencies, and ensure our spatial databases meet rigorous accuracy standards. This image demonstraits a script that used multiprocessing to perform six hundred thousand edits in less than 10 minutes. The result is a GIS system our engineers and field crews can trust for real-time decision-making.

I also design and maintain ArcGIS Enterprise applications tailored to PGW’s operational workflows. These include interactive dashboards, field collection tools, and web maps that visualize asset conditions, pipeline maintenance status, and service requests. Each application is built to streamline how departments interact with geospatial data—making it faster to locate assets, assess field conditions, and report updates.

I lead our GNSS-enabled field data collection program, integrating sub-meter accuracy data from field crews into our enterprise database. By pairing GNSS technology with mobile GIS tools, we’ve significantly improved positional precision and reduced post-processing time— leading to faster updates and improved pipeline traceability.

To support operational visibility, I’ve developed custom dashboards and data quality audits that track project progress, highlight anomalies, and automate data verification steps. These systems provide near real-time insight into work orders, construction activities, and asset updates—ensuring PGW’s spatial data remains complete and compliant.
