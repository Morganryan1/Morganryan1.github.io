Current Role: Senior GIS Analyst – Philadelphia Gas Works

At Philadelphia Gas Works (PGW), I lead geospatial data initiatives that support critical infrastructure management, field operations, and regulatory compliance across the city’s natural gas network. My work focuses on building automated, data-driven solutions that enhance accuracy, speed, and collaboration within PGW’s enterprise GIS ecosystem.

A major focus of my role is automating data collection and cleaning processes using Python, JavaScript, and ArcGIS Enterprise tools. These automations reduce manual data entry, detect and correct geometry or schema inconsistencies, and ensure our spatial databases meet rigorous accuracy standards. The result is a GIS system our engineers and field crews can trust for real-time decision-making.

I also design and maintain ArcGIS Enterprise applications tailored to PGW’s operational workflows. These include interactive dashboards, field collection tools, and web maps that visualize asset conditions, pipeline maintenance status, and service requests. Each application is built to streamline how departments interact with geospatial data—making it faster to locate assets, assess field conditions, and report updates.

As part of PGW’s Geographic Information Office, I oversee the integrity and accessibility of our Enterprise GIS environment, ensuring that spatial data flows seamlessly between engineering, field, and IT teams. This includes managing user permissions, maintaining server health, and optimizing data interoperability between Esri and non-Esri systems.

I also lead our GNSS-enabled field data collection program, which integrates sub-meter accuracy data from field crews into our enterprise database. By pairing GNSS technology with mobile GIS tools, we’ve significantly improved positional precision and reduced post-processing time. This has led to faster updates, fewer data conflicts, and improved pipeline traceability.

To support operational visibility, I’ve developed custom dashboards and data quality audits that track project progress, highlight anomalies, and automate data verification steps. These systems provide near real-time insight into work orders, construction activities, and asset updates—ensuring that PGW’s spatial data remains complete, compliant, and current.

My role also extends to mentorship and collaboration. I supervise GIS technicians responsible for data editing and field verification, helping standardize editing protocols and ensure on-time project delivery. I also coordinate with engineers, IT specialists, and compliance officers to align GIS workflows with organizational objectives—supporting everything from pipeline maintenance and asset management to regulatory audits and safety planning.

By combining scripting automation, field mobility, and system integration, I’m helping PGW modernize how spatial data supports utility operations—reducing redundancy, improving accuracy, and empowering teams to make better, faster, data-backed decisions.
